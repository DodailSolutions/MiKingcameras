## 9. Recommendations for New Platform

1. **Preserve all existing business information** — do not fabricate
2. **Clearly distinguish new vs pre-owned** products
3. **Implement comprehensive product management** with all specifications
4. **Build mobile-first** — most traffic likely from mobile
5. **Integrate Indian payment gateways** (Razorpay, Cashfree)
6. **Indian shipping providers** (Shiprocket, Delhivery, Blue Dart)
7. **GST-compliant invoicing** is mandatory for India
8. **Pincode-based serviceability** is expected by Indian shoppers
9. **WhatsApp is primary customer communication** channel
10. **EMI options** are important for high-value camera purchases
